📌 Principal Display Panel (PDP) Dimension Formula (Rule 7):

  • Rectangular Package: Total area of height × width of the front display face.
  • Cylindrical / Round Container: 40% of the product of height × circumference of the container.
  • Any Other Shape: 20% of the total surface area of the package.

Unit Sale Price (USP) Statutory Guidelines — Rule 6(11)

Mandatory clause for price transparency to prevent deceptive packaging sizing

1. Weight Based Goods (< 1 kg):

If net weight is less than 1 kg, Unit Sale Price must be declared per gram (e.g. ₹0.25 / g or ₹25 / 100g).

2. Weight Based Goods (≥ 1 kg):

If net weight is 1 kg or more, Unit Sale Price must be declared per kilogram (e.g. ₹250.00 / kg).

3. Volume Based Liquids (< 1 L):

If net volume is less than 1 litre, declared per millilitre (e.g. ₹0.30 / ml).

4. Items Sold by Number / Piece:

If sold by number, declared per piece/item (e.g. ₹15.00 / N or ₹15.00 / piece).

Penal Provisions under Legal Metrology Act, 2009

Statutory fines, imprisonment terms, and compounding of contraventions

Enforcement Matrix
Section 36(1) Non-Standard Pack

Penalty for manufacturing, packing, importing, selling, distributing, or delivering pre-packaged commodities not conforming to declarations.

• 1st Offence: Fine up to ₹25,000

• 2nd Offence: Fine up to ₹50,000

• Subsequent: Fine up to ₹1,00,000 or Imprisonment up to 1 Year

Section 36(2) Shortage in Quantity

Penalty for error in net quantity beyond Maximum Permissible Error (MPE) limits prescribed in First Schedule.

• 1st Offence: Fine from ₹10,000 to ₹50,000

• Subsequent: Imprisonment up to 1 Year with fine

Section 38 General Penalty

Penalty for general contravention of provisions of the Act and Rules where no express penalty is otherwise provided.

• Standard Fine: Fine up to ₹5,000

Section 49 Offences by Companies

Vicarious liability holding Directors, Managing Directors, Company Secretary, and nominated managers personally responsible for contraventions.

• Mandatory nomination of responsible Director under Rule 29
